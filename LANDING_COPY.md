## 【3 · WHAT IT DOES】 (feature sections)

### ⚡ Capture a hand in seconds
- **Tap-based, action-first flow** — build the action in order, claim your seat when it reaches you, drop in your cards. No fiddly forms.
- **Only legal actions shown** — a real pot-limit engine drives the buttons, so the math (raise-to sizing, pot-limit max) is always correct.
- **Quick bet sizes** — Fold / Call / Pot for cash; 2×/2.5×/3×/Pot preflop and 2bb/2.5bb/3bb postflop for tournaments.
- **Discreet** — one-handed, dark, fast. Designed to disappear at the table.

### 🎰 Built for how live PLO actually plays
- **8-max, with straddles** — button and UTG straddles handled correctly, in the order they actually act.
- **Cash and tournaments** — including **BB antes** by the real WSOP pot-limit rule.
- **Full hands in tournaments** — preflop through flop, turn, river, and showdown, with villains' shown cards.
- **Honest about partial info** — approximate stacks and partial showdowns are first-class; it never invents data you didn't see.

### 🃏 A table that looks like the table
- A clean **vertical racetrack felt** with a brass rail, your seat anchored, and a pulsing marker on whoever's turn it is.
- Live **pot, SPR, bet chips, dealer button**, your hole cards in front of your seat, the board across the felt, and villains' cards at showdown.

### ▶️ Replay every hand
- **Step through any saved hand** action by action, or hit **autoplay** (1× / 2× / 4×).
- Flip the display between **dollars, big blinds, or chips**.

### 🔗 Share a hand with anyone
- **Shareable replay links** — the entire hand rides inside the link. Send it in a text or Discord; they tap it and watch it play in their browser. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Export a clip** — download a crisp **MP4** (or GIF) of the replay that plays anywhere.

### 📅 Track every session
- **Buy in, add on, cash out** — your win rate comes from real results, whether or not you logged a single hand.
- **A calendar of your year** — hours and +/- on every day you played, with a month-end ledger: days played, best day, worst day, net, running total.
- **Win rate you can trust** — $/hr and bb/hr, plus an honest read on how sure that number is and how many more hours it needs. Breakdowns by stakes, venue, day of week, and session length.
- **Tournaments too** — ROI, in-the-money rate, buy-ins and cashes.
- **All-in luck** — see how your captured all-ins ran against the equity when the money went in.
- **PLO5 and mixed games** can be tracked alongside your PLO.

### 🧮 Equity calculator — 4 and 5 card
- **PLO and PLO5**, up to six players, any board — leave a hand blank to run it against a random one.
- **Exact on the flop and turn**, a 100,000-deal simulation preflop — and it always tells you which.

### 🗂️ Sessions, organized
- **Resume your current session in one tap** and keep logging; **end it** when you rack up.
- A hand list where every hand is labeled by position, cards, and suit shape (e.g. *MP · AAKK ds*).

### 📵 Yours, on your device
- **Works offline** — sessions and hands are stored locally on your phone. No account, nothing sent to a server. Download a backup any time.
- **Installs like a native app** — add it to your home screen and it opens full-screen.
