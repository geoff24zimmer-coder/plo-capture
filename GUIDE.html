Hand History — Quick Start

Fast, discreet capture of live 4-card PLO hands. Cash & tournament.

hands.theploshow.com

★ The one thing that works differently
You do not pick your seat first. You replay the table's action in order — starting with the first player to act — tapping what each player did. When the amber pointer reaches your spot, tap This is me — [position] and enter your four cards. That's the whole trick.

Log your first hand

  1. On the home screen tap Cash Game (or Tournament). Enter the venue, blinds, and table size, then Start.
  2. On New hand, the blinds and stacks are pre-filled (100bb by default). For cash, pick a straddle if there was one — No straddle / UTG / Button — then tap Start hand.
  3. The table appears with an amber pointer on the player to act. Tap what they did — Fold, Call, or Raise — going around the table. The app only offers the legal pot-limit options, so you can't mis-enter a raise size.
  4. When the pointer reaches your seat, tap This is me — [position] and pick your four cards.
  5. When the hand resolves, tap Save hand. You're dropped back on a fresh setup for the next one.

The whole hand, every time

Cash & Tournament  Full hand

Every hand plays out completely. You're prompted for the flop, turn, and river cards as the streets come, then tap the winning seat at showdown. Didn't see a card? Cancel the picker — partial info is fine.

Tournament extras

Tournaments also capture the level, BB ante, and players-left (for ICM later). Cash hands capture straddles — UTG or button.

Handy controls

  • Undo (top-right ↶) — removes the last action you entered.
  • $ / BB toggle (top-right) — show bets in money or in big blinds.
  • Save spot — once the action reaches you, bank just your decision point mid-hand (for solver review) instead of playing it out.
  • Flag for review — star a hand to find it again later.

After the hand

  • Hand list — every hand with your net result (green up / red down). Tap one to replay it on the animated table.
  • Share / save a replay — in the replayer, share a hand as a link (it replays on any phone, no app needed) or Download an MP4/GIF clip to post.
  • ⋮ on a hand → Export for review, or Delete (long-press also deletes).
  • Export for solver (top-right ⬆) — bundles the session's hands for the Monker Killer solver.
  • End — closes out the session; it stays available under Past sessions.

The end game — your hands feed the solver

★ Why you’re capturing
Every hand you log is solver-ready. The point isn’t just a tidy history — it’s to drop your real spots into a PLO solver and get back optimal strategy for the exact situations you face at your table. Two paths:
  • Export for solver (a session’s ⬆ button) bundles your completed hands into population frequencies the solver reads.
  • Save spot → Copy spot JSON sends a single decision straight into the solver’s Import Hand for a one-off GTO answer.
Capture now, solve later — the data is built to load in directly, with no re-entry.

Install it on your phone

Open hands.theploshow.com in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android) → Share / ⋮ → Add to Home Screen. It launches full-screen like a native app.

Goal: a logged hand takes about 15 seconds of quiet tapping.  ·  Your hands are stored on that device — there's no login or cloud sync yet, so clearing the browser's data clears the hands. Use the share link or MP4 export to keep any you care about.
